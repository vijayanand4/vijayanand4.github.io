01 — Profile

About Me

I am a Cybersecurity & GRC Leader with deep expertise in securing India's financial sector — from the National Operations Centre of the country's largest bank to the Information Security function at the Reserve Bank of India. I hold a CISSP and 9 additional certifications spanning Zero Trust, AppSec, network security, privacy, and ITIL.

My work sits at the intersection of technology and governance: designing risk-based security frameworks, directing SOC/NOC operations, leading major incident investigations, and embedding operational resilience across large mission-critical environments. I have managed teams of 25+ professionals and governed infrastructure supporting 25,000+ branches of national-scale financial services.

I am a Gold Medalist M.Tech from IIITM Gwalior with a published research paper in wireless sensor network routing. I write regularly on cybersecurity topics for practitioners — covering incident response, vulnerability management, PKI, resilience engineering, and regulatory compliance for Indian financial institutions.
