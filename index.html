01 — Profile

About Me

I am a Cybersecurity & GRC Leader with deep expertise in securing India's financial sector — from the Resilience Operations Centre of the country's largest bank to the Information Security function at the Reserve Bank of India, where I serve as Assistant General Manager. I hold a CISSP and 9 additional certifications spanning Zero Trust architecture, application security, network security, privacy operations, and ITIL.

My work sits at the intersection of technology and governance: designing risk-based security frameworks, directing SOC & NOC operations, leading major incident investigations and root cause analyses, and embedding operational resilience across large, mission-critical environments. I have managed teams of 25+ professionals and governed infrastructure supporting 25,000+ bank branches and national financial services.

I hold an M.Tech (Gold Medal) from the Indian Institute of Information Technology & Management, Gwalior, with a published research paper in wireless sensor network routing. I write regularly on cybersecurity for practitioners — covering incident response, vulnerability prioritisation, PKI, resilience engineering, and banking-sector regulatory compliance.
